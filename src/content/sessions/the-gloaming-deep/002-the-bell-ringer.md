We followed the sound of the bell through three flooded galleries, wading chest-deep, holding the last torch above our heads like a holy relic.

## The Belfry Below

The bell hung in a shaft that should not exist: a tower built downward, into the earth. Something pale and long-fingered clung to the rope.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Maren's arrow went wide. The thing laughed. |
| 2 | Brother Ash's turning failed. His god was not listening down here. |
| 3 | Tobin grabbed the rope to stop the ringing. |

Tobin was pulled up into the dark of the shaft. We heard the bell ring once more, then nothing.

## Aftermath

We took his pack, his lucky die, and the half-map he had been drawing. Quill swears the map shows rooms we have not visited yet.
