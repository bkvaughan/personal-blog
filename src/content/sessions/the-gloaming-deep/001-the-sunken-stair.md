The rain had not stopped for nine days when the abbot's steward found us in the Gutter & Gaff, and by then none of us were in a position to refuse silver.

## The Steward's Offer

Fifty gold apiece to recover the **Reliquary of Saint Veyle** from the lower crypts. The steward would not say why the brothers could not fetch it themselves. Brother Ash noticed he never once looked toward the abbey while he spoke.

> "The bells ring at night now. No one pulls the ropes."

## Down the Stair

Two torches between four of us. The stair spiralled into black water at the forty-third step; Quill counted. Tobin went first with a rope about his waist and came back up white-lipped, swearing something had brushed his leg.

---

We found the first chamber dry, its walls carved with saints whose faces had been chiselled away. On the altar: a single candle, still warm.

The first torch guttered out just as we heard the bell.
